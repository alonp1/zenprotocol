How it works

  1. Make: the maker locks the asset in the contract and states what they want in return.
  2. Take: anyone pays the wanted asset and receives the locked one, in the same transaction. A part-fill leaves the remainder as a new order.
  3. Cancel: the maker signs and gets the locked asset back.
