Prototype for experiments. One operator holds the reserves. Test networks only.

How it works

  1. Link. Sign a message with your EVM key that links it to your Zen address.
  2. Deposit. Send USDC to the bridge address. After confirmations, the same amount of the token arrives at your Zen address.
  3. Withdraw. Send the token to the bridge and name an EVM address. The bridge destroys the tokens and pays out USDC.

Deposit and withdraw from this page are not built yet; today they use the scripts described in BRIDGE.md.
