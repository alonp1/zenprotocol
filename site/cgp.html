Each interval is 10,000 blocks. The balance at the snapshot block is the vote weight; nominations follow the snapshot, then the allocation and payout vote. The allocation (the CGP share of every block reward; miners get the rest) decided in an interval applies to the next one. Interval numbers as in the community wallets (the node counts 24 more). A ballot is counted if it is the first of its key and type inside its voting window.
Indexed from the chain by the community node of this site, updated every 5 minutes. Community-run. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Zen Protocol Ltd.
