CGP voting history

The CGP (Common Goods Pool) is the community fund of ZP. In every interval, ZP holders vote on two things: how each block reward is split between miners and the fund, and who the fund pays.

How a CGP interval works
  1. Snapshot. At the snapshot block, every wallet's ZP balance is recorded. That balance is its vote weight for this interval; coins moved later do not count.
  2. Nominations. Holders propose who should be paid from the fund and how much.
  3. Voting. Holders vote on the reward split and choose one of the nominated payouts. Only the first ballot of each wallet per vote type counts.
  4. Result. The payout with the most weight is paid from the fund just after the interval ends, and the new split applies to the whole next interval. The miners' share may move by at most 15% of itself per interval, and never below 10%.
